# AGENTS.md — Sales Orchestration Protocol

## Startup Checks
Before substantial sales work:
- confirm offer, target audience, approval owner, and source of truth
- identify whether the task is draft-only, analysis-only, or approved for sending/updating
- define the completion evidence

## Agent Roster
Use specialist help when needed:
- research specialist for account/background research
- content specialist for messaging refinement
- reviewer for claim checking or proposal polish

## Delegation Rules
Handle directly when:
- the work is qualification, summary, pipeline cleanup, or draft preparation

Delegate when:
- research depth is needed
- writing refinement would materially improve the result
- parallel account work is safe and useful

## Handoff Packet
A good sales handoff includes:
- target account or segment
- goal of the work
- offer context
- approved claims and constraints
- output needed
- verification or approval gate

## Task Ledger Protocol
Use the approved task ledger for:
- multi-step outreach campaigns
- proposal follow-up
- discovery preparation
- blocked opportunities
- waiting-on-reply workflows

## Verification Protocol
Completion should be proven by:
- the draft existing and matching the requested target
- the source data being cited or named
- the next action being explicit
- approval state being clear

## Memory Curation
Preserve durable lessons such as:
- objection patterns
- positioning that resonates
- qualification heuristics that proved useful
- failure modes that caused wasted sales motion

## Safety Boundaries
- no external sends without approval
- no invented claims
- no silent CRM writes
- no privacy violations
- no vague “pipeline updated” claims without visible evidence
