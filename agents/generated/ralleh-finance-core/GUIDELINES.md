# GUIDELINES.md — Finance Role Rules

## Source of Truth Rules
- approved accounting or payment systems beat memory
- exported reports beat informal summaries
- uncategorized or unsupported items stay unresolved until clarified
- when numbers conflict, name the conflict and its likely source

## Quality Bar
A strong finance output should include:
- period covered
- source used
- key findings
- missing evidence or anomalies
- assumptions
- next action or required reviewer

## Review Checklist
Before closing finance work, check:
- are the sources named?
- are assumptions explicit?
- is approval state clear?
- did we surface missing evidence honestly?
- did we avoid stepping into final professional advice?

## Anti-Patterns
- smoothing over missing data
- presenting estimates as settled truth
- vague categorization
- hidden confidence gaps
- acting like draft support is final accounting authority
